## User Scenarios & Testing _(mandatory)_

### User Story 1 - Conquest Loop: Capture a Flag, Spawn Map Flips (Priority: P1)

A player walks into a control point's capture radius, watches the capture meter neutralize then flip the flag, and — the moment the CP crosses to fully captured — every teammate on the deploy screen sees the flag appear as a spawn option while every enemy sees it vanish, instantly and unambiguously.

**Why this priority**: This is the pillar that gives the other two something to coordinate about (CQ-2/CQ-3/CQ-4). The spawn-ownership flip is the feature; the atomic flip event is a correctness requirement, not polish (PRD Appendix B.4). A graybox match with capture, flips, tickets, and bleed is a viable — if lonely — game with no squads or voice at all.

**Independent Test**: In a bot-filled 16v16 graybox match, capture CP B and verify (a) two-stage neutralize-then-capture meter behavior at the tuned rates, (b) the deploy screen flips ownership within one RTT for all clients, (c) tickets drain by respawn and bleed per the tuning table, and (d) a team reaching 0 tickets loses.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an enemy-held CP and one attacker in its capture radius with no defenders, **When** the attacker remains in radius, **Then** the meter moves at 6.25 pts/s (neutralize in 16 s, full flip in 32 s), scaling 1.5× with 2 attackers and capping at 2.0× with 3+ (CQ-2).
2. **Given** both teams have players in the capture radius, **When** the meter is contested, **Then** capture progress freezes until one side clears the radius (CQ-2).
3. **Given** a CP crosses to fully captured at server tick T, **When** the server broadcasts the flip, **Then** a single atomic `{cpId, newOwner, tick}` event goes out on the reliable stream, queued spawns on that CP are invalidated in the same tick, and no client can complete a spawn at the lost flag after T (CQ-3, Appendix B.4).
4. **Given** a player mid-spawn-countdown on a CP their team just lost, **When** the flip lands, **Then** they are bounced back to spawn selection with a map flash + audio sting + banner (CQ-3).
5. **Given** a team holding ≤2 of 5 CPs, **When** time passes, **Then** that team bleeds 1 ticket/5 s; at zero CPs, 1 ticket/2 s; and each respawn costs 1 ticket (CQ-4). First team to 0 tickets loses.

### User Story 2 - Squad-Leader Spawn with Combat Denial (Priority: P1)

A player joins a squad from the deploy screen, selects the squad-leader icon as their spawn point, and spawns 1–3 m behind the leader's facing with brief spawn protection — unless the leader took damage in the last 8 seconds, in which case leader spawn is denied and the player must pick a controlled flag.

**Why this priority**: The mobile spawn is the social mechanic the whole design hangs on (SQ-1..4); the north-star metric (≥50% of spawns on leader) measures exactly this loop. Independently testable on top of US1's deploy screen without voice, classes, or vehicles.

**Independent Test**: With two clients in a squad, kill client A, spawn A on leader B, verify placement and spawn-protection shimmer; damage B and verify leader spawn is denied for exactly 8 s (with both visibility variants of the denial state, per the server flag).

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a player without a squad, **When** they open the one-key squad menu, **Then** they can create a squad (becoming leader), join an open squad (up to 6 members), or remain lone wolf with spawns restricted to controlled flags (SQ-1, SQ-6).
2. **Given** a dead squad member and a living, undamaged leader, **When** the member selects the leader spawn, **Then** they spawn 1–3 m behind the leader's facing with a spawn-protection shimmer lasting 1.5 s or until they fire/move aggressively (SQ-2).
3. **Given** the leader took damage 3 s ago, **When** a member attempts leader spawn, **Then** the spawn is denied and the deploy screen shows the combat-denied state — a visible countdown or an opaque "unavailable" indicator depending on the SQ-4 playtest flag — until 8 s have elapsed since the last damage (SQ-4).
4. **Given** the leader is dead, **When** members view the deploy screen, **Then** leader spawn is unavailable until the leader respawns (SQ-3).
5. **Given** the leader leaves the match, **When** succession triggers, **Then** leadership auto-passes to the longest-tenured member; manual hand-off is also available (SQ-3).

### User Story 3 - Squad Voice: Auto-Joined Channel + Command Net (Priority: P2)

A player joins a squad and is in its voice channel with zero configuration — push-to-talk by default, speaking indicators on the roster and above heads. The squad leader holds a second PTT key to talk on the command channel with other leaders.

**Why this priority**: Voice is the coordination medium (VC-1..5), but the game is playable (and Phase 1 ships) without it; it is Phase 2's centerpiece. Independently testable against a live SFU with two squads of test clients.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a player joins a squad, **When** the join completes, **Then** their client is connected to that squad's voice channel with no configuration, PTT by default with an open-mic option and per-player mute (VC-1).
2. **Given** a squad member speaks, **When** their audio transmits, **Then** a speaking indicator shows on the squad roster and their name flashes above their head for teammates (VC-4).
3. **Given** a squad leader, **When** they hold the command-net PTT key, **Then** their audio reaches all squad leaders (and the commander, once that role ships) and not their own squad channel (VC-2).
4. **Given** a client with a voice token for squad X in match M, **When** it attempts to join squad Y's channel or a channel in another match, **Then** the SFU rejects the join — tokens are JWTs scoped to match + squad ID (VC-3, Appendix A.4).
5. **Given** a disruptive member, **When** the leader voice-kicks them or a player reports them, **Then** the kick removes them from the channel and the report enters the moderation flow; server-level mute lists apply across matches (VC-5).

---

### User Story 4 - Classes & Medic Revive: Winning the Attrition War (Priority: P2)

A player picks one of four classes on deploy. When a squadmate goes down, a Medic revives them inside the 10 s bleed-out window — and the team's ticket pool does not pay for a respawn.

**Why this priority**: The revive-preserves-tickets rule is the mechanical reason squads that stick together win the attrition war (Appendix B.2); classes give squads internal texture. Requires US1's ticket system but not voice or vehicles.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the deploy screen, **When** a player selects a class, **Then** the four MVP classes are available: Assault (rifle + grenades), Medic (heal/revive), Support (LMG + ammo resupply), Recon (marksman + spotting).
2. **Given** a downed player within the 10 s bleed-out window, **When** a Medic revives them, **Then** they return to play and **zero tickets** are drained; if the window lapses or they give up, the subsequent respawn costs 1 ticket.
3. **Given** a Recon (or any player) aiming at a visible enemy, **When** they press the spot key (Q), **Then** a 3D marker pins that enemy for their squad for 6 s.
4. **Given** any player sprinting, **When** stamina depletes, **Then** sprint ends — deliberate BF2-weight movement, no slide-canceling or advanced movement tech; TTK is moderate (3–5 body shots).

---

### User Story 5 - Mount Up: Squad Transport Between Flags (Priority: P3)

At round start, a 4-seat open-top truck waits at the home base. A squad mounts up together and crosses the map in ~35 s instead of ~90 s on foot, dismounting to assault a far-lane flag — at 32v32 scale.

**Why this priority**: The transport exists at MVP purely to prove vehicle netcode and give squads a reason to move as a unit (PRD §5); 32v32 is the Phase 3 product-target scale-up. Everything above ships and is testable at 16v16 without it.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a truck with free seats, **When** up to 4 players interact with it, **Then** each occupies a distinct seat (one driver) and the vehicle's movement replicates to all clients under the same prediction/interpolation regime as infantry.
2. **Given** a squad mounted at their home base, **When** the driver crosses the map, **Then** base→far-lane travel takes ~35 s versus ~90 s on foot (Appendix C travel-time targets).
3. **Given** a 32v32 match (64 players), **When** the match runs, **Then** per-client downstream bandwidth stays ≤40 KB/s and the server holds the 30 Hz tick — no design regression from the 16v16 numbers (NFR-001, NFR-002).

### Edge Cases

- **Leader disconnects mid-combat-denial**: succession (SQ-3) passes leadership while the 8 s denial clock is live — the new leader's own damage state governs; the old timer does not transfer.
- **Simultaneous capture contention at the flip boundary**: two teams' meters cannot both cross ±100 in one tick; the server tick is authoritative and exactly one `{cpId, newOwner, tick}` event is emitted (Appendix B.4).
- **WebTransport blocked (pre-26.4 Safari, UDP-hostile firewalls)**: client falls back to WebSocket with a degraded-but-playable experience; the server treats fallback clients identically (Appendix A.1).
- **Voice SFU unreachable**: the match plays on — voice is an enhancement layer; the roster shows a channel-unavailable state and reconnection is attempted in the background.
- **All squad slots full**: a joining player becomes lone wolf (controlled-flag spawns only) and may create a new squad if the team squad cap allows.
- **Spawn-protection abuse**: the 1.5 s shimmer ends immediately on firing or aggressive movement (SQ-2) so protected players cannot deal damage while immune.
- **Ticket race at match end**: if both teams would hit 0 in the same tick, the team with more remaining tickets before that tick's drain wins; a true tie is resolved by CP count.

## Requirements _(mandatory)_

### Functional Requirements

PRD requirement IDs are preserved verbatim; the PRD (`combined-arms-prd.md`) is authoritative for elaboration.

**Pillar 1 — Squad System with Mobile Spawn**

- **SQ-1**: Squads MUST hold up to 6 players; players MUST be able to create a squad (becoming leader), join an open squad, or play lone wolf with spawns restricted to controlled flags.
- **SQ-2**: Any squad member MUST be able to spawn on the leader's position when the leader is alive and not combat-denied; spawn placement is 1–3 m behind the leader's facing with a 1.5 s spawn-protection shimmer (ends early on firing/aggressive movement).
- **SQ-3**: Leadership MUST auto-pass to the longest-tenured member when the leader leaves; manual hand-off MUST be supported; leader death MUST disable squad spawn until the leader respawns.
- **SQ-4**: Squad spawn MUST be disabled for 8 seconds after the leader takes damage. Both denial-state presentations (visible countdown vs. opaque) MUST be implemented behind a server flag and instrumented for playtest comparison (per Clarifications).
- **SQ-5**: Squad leaders MUST have a squad-visible map icon, an Attack/Defend/Move order system (map marker or 3D world ping), and passive score bonuses when squadmates act on orders.
- **SQ-6**: A persistent left-edge squad roster (names, health state, alive/dead, class icon, leader star) and a one-key squad menu MUST be provided.

**Pillar 2 — Squad Voice Channels**

- **VC-1**: Joining a squad MUST auto-join its voice channel with zero configuration; PTT default, open-mic option, per-player mute.
- **VC-2**: Two-tier comms MUST exist: squad channel (all 6) and command channel (squad leaders + future commander) on a second PTT key.
- **VC-3**: Voice MUST be WebRTC via an SFU (LiveKit or mediasoup class), one uplink per client, SFU co-located with the game region.
- **VC-4**: Speaking indicators MUST appear on the squad roster and above teammate heads.
- **VC-5**: Moderation baseline MUST ship with voice: leader voice-kick, server-level mute list, report flow.
- **VC-6**: Positional/proximity voice MUST NOT ship at MVP (explicitly excluded; it dilutes the squad-channel design).

**Pillar 3 — Conquest**

- **CQ-1**: The MVP map MUST contain 5 CPs plus uncapturable home bases (NW and SE corners, per the north-shore Clarification); the system MUST support 5–7 CPs and 0–2 home bases per team for future maps.
- **CQ-2**: CPs MUST use a −100…+100 capture meter with headcount-scaled rates (6.25 pts/s base; 1.0×/1.5×/2.0× at 1/2/3+ net players), contested freeze, two-stage neutralize-then-capture, 20–30 m radii, and 2 pts/s decay toward the owner's state when attackers leave.
- **CQ-3**: A fully-captured CP MUST atomically flip spawn ownership: appear for the capturing team, disappear for the loser, bounce mid-countdown spawners, and announce via map flash + audio sting + banner. The flip event `{cpId, newOwner, tick}` MUST be broadcast on the reliable stream and invalidate queued spawns in the same server tick.
- **CQ-4**: Teams MUST start with 300 tickets (16v16 baseline); respawns cost 1 ticket (revives cost zero); majority bleed 1 ticket/5 s when holding fewer than half the CPs; total-control bleed 1 ticket/2 s at zero CPs; first team to 0 loses.
- **CQ-5**: The deploy screen MUST show a top-down map with selectable owned CPs + squad-leader icon, and a 10 s spawn-wave cadence so squads re-enter together.
- **CQ-6**: A classic-CTF secondary mode on the same maps is Phase 3 scope — planned, explicitly not the flagship.

**Supporting Systems**

- **FR-001 (Classes)**: Four MVP classes MUST ship: Assault, Medic (heal/revive; revives inside the 10 s bleed-out window preserve tickets), Support (LMG + ammo resupply), Recon (marksman + spotting). Anti-tank and Engineer arrive with vehicles (post-MVP).
- **FR-002 (Spotting)**: Aim + Q MUST ping the targeted enemy for the player's squad as a 3D marker lasting 6 s.
- **FR-003 (Movement)**: Movement MUST be deliberate BF2-weight — sprint with stamina, no slide-canceling or advanced movement tech; TTK 3–5 body shots.
- **FR-004 (Transport)**: One open-top 4-seat truck MUST ship at MVP to prove vehicle netcode and enable squad movement between flags (base→far-lane ~35 s vs ~90 s on foot).
- **FR-005 (Map)**: The MVP map MUST be the real North Chattanooga north-shore ~600×600 m box (Coolidge Park / Frazier Ave), built from the digital-twin bake pipeline: baked `terrain.json` as heightmap; baked streets/buildings informing cover and CP placement. CP assignment: A = Coolidge Park carousel lawn, B = Walnut St Bridge north landing, C = elevated hill-climb block between Frazier and the ridge, D = Frazier Ave commercial strip, E = Renaissance Park knoll. The committed site config `sites/north-shore.json` is a distinct ticket.
- **FR-006 (Bots)**: Phase 1 MUST include bots sufficient to fill a 16v16 server for testing the Conquest loop.

### Non-Functional Requirements

- **NFR-001 (Bandwidth)**: Downstream per client MUST be ≤20 KB/s at 32 players and ≤40 KB/s at 64, via delta compression, quantization (bit-packed ArrayBuffers, not JSON), grid-based interest management, and a priority accumulator.
- **NFR-002 (Tick)**: The server MUST run an authoritative 30 Hz fixed tick owning all truth: positions, health, capture meters, tickets.
- **NFR-003 (Latency)**: Perceived input latency MUST be sub-100 ms via client-side prediction + reconciliation (smoothed corrections ~100 ms) and snapshot interpolation (100 ms buffer) for remote entities.
- **NFR-004 (Lag comp)**: Hitscan MUST be lag-compensated by server rewind, capped at 250 ms; projectiles are simulated forward with no rewind.
- **NFR-005 (Transport)**: WebTransport (QUIC) MUST be the primary transport — unreliable datagrams for per-tick state, reliable streams for must-arrive events — with WebSocket fallback for pre-26.4 Safari and QUIC-hostile networks.
- **NFR-006 (Browser-playable)**: The client MUST run in a browser with no install: Three.js/R3F shell for menus/deploy/HUD chrome, plain TS/ECS simulation layer outside React's reconciler, WebGPU renderer with WebGL2 fallback.
- **NFR-007 (Anti-cheat posture)**: Server authority over everything (including capture progress and ammo), server-side sanity checks (speed/RoF caps, impossible-angle detection), statistical outlier flagging into a review queue, and community-server admin kick/ban. No kernel anti-cheat is possible; ranked play is a non-goal.
- **NFR-008 (Voice security/ops)**: Voice tokens MUST be per-match JWTs scoped to match + squad ID; idle channels MUST be torn down aggressively; the VC-5 moderation baseline is a launch requirement for the voice phase, not a fast-follow.
- **NFR-009 (Scale headroom)**: No design decision may preclude 64 players (per Clarifications); 32v32 is the product target reached in Phase 3.

### Key Entities

- **Squad**: up to 6 players, one leader; owns a voice channel, an order marker, and the leader-spawn availability state (alive + not combat-denied).
- **Control Point (CP)**: capture radius, −100…+100 meter, owner, spawn-point cluster; emits atomic ownership-flip events.
- **Ticket Pool**: per-team counter (300 start); drained by respawns and bleed; zero = loss.
- **Match**: one server process; 30 Hz tick; roster of players, squads, CPs, vehicles; per-match voice room set.
- **Player**: team, squad (or lone wolf), class, alive/downed/dead state, spawn selection.
- **Site Config (`sites/north-shore.json`)**: committed digital-twin bake parameters producing the MVP map's terrain and geometry inputs.

## Success Criteria _(mandatory)_

### Measurable Outcomes

PRD §8 metrics, verbatim targets:

- **SC-001 (North star)**: ≥50% of all spawns in a match are squad-leader spawns.
- **SC-002**: ≥70% of players in a match are in squads.
- **SC-003**: ≥40% of squads have at least 2 members using voice in an average match.
- **SC-004**: Median match duration is 15–25 minutes.
- **SC-005**: Comeback rate (team behind at half-tickets wins) is between 15% and 30%.
- **SC-006**: Winner's remaining tickets are ≤40% of the starting pool in the median match (blowouts indicate map/bleed tuning failure).
- **SC-007**: Average match sees ≥8 spawn-ownership flips.
- **SC-008**: Median session ≥2 matches; D7 retention ≥15% for a playtest cohort.

Map-layout instrumentation (PRD Appendix C — the lattice succeeds if):

- **SC-009**: B and D flip 3–4× as often as A and E.
- **SC-010**: C changes hands most of all CPs.
- **SC-011**: Death heatmaps, per-CP flip counts, squad-spawn locations, and time-to-first-contact from each spawn cluster are captured for every playtest match (this instrumentation also feeds the SQ-4 visible-vs-opaque comparison).

## Assumptions

- The digital-twin bake pipeline (#232 parameterized bake, #229 lidar heights) can produce a heightmap + street/building layout for the north-shore box; adapting `terrain.json` to a game-resolution heightmap is feasible plan-level work.
- WebTransport remains Baseline across major browsers (Safari 26.4+ shipped March 2026); WebSocket fallback covers the remainder.
- Node/TypeScript is viable for the authoritative server at 32 players; Rust is the escape hatch if profiling demands it at 64 (Appendix A.4).
- Phase 0's kill criterion is real: if perceived latency is unacceptable in the capsule graybox, platform choice is revisited before any further build-out.
- Game servers, matchmaker, and voice SFU are hosted infrastructure outside GitHub Pages; the browser client itself remains statically exported.

## Out of Scope

From PRD non-goals (§2) and VC-6:

- Jets, helicopters, and armor (vehicles beyond the one light ground transport).
- Commander role with artillery/UAV/supply drops at MVP (Phase 2+; squad leaders come first).
- Ranked progression, unlocks, persistent stats.
- Console/native clients.
- Destructible environments.
- Proximity/positional voice (dilutes the squad-channel design).
- Monetization of any kind (deferred entirely per Clarifications).
