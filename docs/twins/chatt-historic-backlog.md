# Chatt twin: historic rebuild backlog

Generated 2026-09-23 from the research behind [chatt-historic-rebuild.md](chatt-historic-rebuild.md). Epic: #1289. Building #1: #1290.

**The unit of work is a real building, not a Warehouse slug.** The 129 Warehouse models resolve to **215 buildings** plus **31 non-buildings**. 177 buildings sit under at least one Warehouse slug; 38 have no model today and are backlog in their own right. Every one of the 129 slugs appears below exactly where it belongs; one slug can cover several buildings, and several slugs can cover one.

**Ranking.** Ranks 1–2 are fixed: the 1886 Car Barns building, then the standing 1907 Hunt trolley barn across W 3rd St. After those, each tier is ordered by distance from the new spawn at Market & W 3rd (35.0535269, -85.3096573), with demolished or unknown buildings counted at double distance and tier A weighted by significance. Rubric: Unit = one real building. Tiers: A = individually NRHP-listed at any time (incl. delisted/demolished) or part of a listed multi-building property (Car Barns, Sts Peter & Paul, Fountain Square, Warehouse Row), plus seven named district landmarks (Loveman's, Hotel Patten, Southern Express flatiron, Volunteer State Life, Hamilton National Bank 701 Market, the two Patten Pkwy Coca-Cola-site rows). B = district-contributing, or built before 1960 and never rated non-contributing. C = post-1960 or non-rated landmark: 10+ storeys, civic, or a signature design. D = everything else, including anything rated non-contributing. Rank: Car Barns 1886 barn is 1 and the 1907 Hunt barn is 2. Every tier is sorted by distance from Market & W 3rd, with demolished or unknown buildings counted at twice their distance. In tier A that distance is also divided by a weight: 3 for a city landmark, 2 for listed and standing, 1 otherwise. Replaced buildings with nothing to model go last. Actions for non-buildings: model-later = worth an owner model after the building backlog; keep-as-is = no owner model, the OSM extrusion or terrain already in the twin is enough once the #714 GLB is withdrawn; drop = remove the slot or unit.

**Status and NRHP columns are research output, not survey.** Statuses marked standing come from the NRHP list and current OSM, and most were not re-checked on the ground. Every row has at least one source link; treat them as starting points, not checked references. Confirm a row before starting its building, and correct this table when you do.

Map links go to OpenStreetMap. No Google links: [the licence rules](chatt-historic-rebuild.md#licence-rules-for-every-building) keep Google imagery out of modelling.
