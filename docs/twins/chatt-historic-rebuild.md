# Chatt twin: the historic rebuild

**Epic:** #1289 · **Building #1:** #1290 (Chattanooga Car Barns) · **Licence background:** #714 · **Written:** 2026-09-23

The owner is re-modelling, by hand, every building the `/chatt` twin borrows from 3D Warehouse, historic buildings first. This file is the plan. [chatt-historic-backlog.md](chatt-historic-backlog.md) is the ranked list of buildings with sources for each. [chatt-historic-rebuild-primer.md](chatt-historic-rebuild-primer.md) is the prompt to paste at the start of a modelling session. [car-barns/](car-barns/) is building #1.

## Why

`public/twins/chatt/models/` ships 129 GLBs downloaded from 3D Warehouse. Their General Model License forbids use "in a mapping or geographic application" and standalone redistribution, and a public repo rendering them at true coordinates does both (#714). Nobody has asked Trimble for permission. Replacing the models with the owner's own geometry cures the problem one building at a time, and it gives each building a documented history instead of an anonymous 2010-era upload.

## What is decided

- **The unit of work is a real building, not a Warehouse slug.** The 129 slugs resolve to 215 buildings plus 31 non-buildings. One slug can cover a block, and three slugs can model one tower.
- **One GitHub issue per building, opened when it is started.** The epic holds the ranked list, so nothing sits open that nobody is working on.
- **Building #1 is the Chattanooga Car Barns**: the 1886-87 stable and car barn at Market & W 3rd, which was CARTA's bus garage until 1978 and was demolished from July 2025. It is modelled as it stood in October 1978 (recommended; the era is the owner's call in #1290).
- **The twin's walk spawn is now at Market × W 3rd**, facing the Car Barns site with the standing 1907 Hunt trolley barn across the street. See [Start points](#start-points).
- **Geometry comes only from sources the licence rules allow.** See [Licence rules](#licence-rules-for-every-building).

## The workflow, per building

```
dossier ─► measured shell ─► SketchUp scaffold ─► owner models it ─► DAE ─► GLB ─► authored slot in the twin
 (NRHP,     (USGS LiDAR:       (ada-stair-generator   (by hand, over     (convert-scan.mjs)   (emit-models.ts,
  Sanborn,   footprint,         site pipeline)         the scaffold)                            credits, overrides)
  photos)    roof, grade)
```

1. **Open the building's issue** and confirm its backlog row. Status, NRHP number and sources are research output, not survey.
2. **Dossier.** The NRHP nomination (NPGallery), Sanborn sheets (Library of Congress), archival photos, the county parcel. The Car Barns brief, [car-barns/BRIEF.md](car-barns/BRIEF.md), is the pattern: every claim cites a file or URL, and a second pass tries to refute the first.
3. **Measured shell from USGS 3DEP LiDAR**, which is public domain. Three flights cover downtown:
   - **TN_HamiltonCounty_B25**, April 2025, QL1, about 11 pts/m². The densest, and it caught buildings demolished later in 2025.
   - **TN_27County_blk4_2015**, December 2015. This is the flight the twin's building heights come from.
   - **TN_HAMILTONCOUNTY_2011**, which has no CRS header: read it as EPSG:26916 in metres.

   Fit the footprint, rotation, grade, roof plane and parapet profile. For the Car Barns this is [car-barns/massing_spec.json](car-barns/massing_spec.json).

4. **SketchUp scaffold** through the site pipeline in the owner's `ada-stair-generator` repo, which is private. That pipeline already fits LiDAR shells, solves cameras, gates quality (G0–G6) and keeps read-only `.skp` waypoints it calls _spawn points_. Its multi-site support is being merged as this is written; start a new site after that lands. For a **demolished** building, the Street View stages do not apply: the Static API serves only the newest capture, which shows a construction site, and Google imagery is excluded anyway. They are replaced by archival-photo camera solves, controlled by LiDAR corners.
5. **The owner models the building by hand** over the scaffold, in true-north metres, with the origin on a surveyed corner. Model it axis-aligned and record the rotation as `yawDeg`. Faces go white side out, because the twin renders single-sided. No ground plane, and no basement: the model is seated on the terrain by its lowest point.
6. **Export** COLLADA, then run `docker compose exec scripthammer node scripts/house/convert-scan.mjs --dae <file.dae> --out <slug>.glb`. It prints the bounding box in metres; check it against the measured footprint. Budget: 24k LOD0 triangles, 3 MB, 8 materials, 2 textures (`scripts/warehouse/__tests__/budget.test.ts`).
7. **Into the twin**, using the replace-a-slot recipe [below](#replacing-a-warehouse-slot). Verify in `/chatt/?diorama&walk`, which is the only renderer that draws models today.

## Licence rules for every building

The point is to cure #714, not to swap it for a different licence problem. The rules below come from a licence review of each input, done 2026-09-23. It is not legal advice.

- **Derive geometry only from public-domain inputs:** USGS 3DEP LiDAR and DEMs, Library of Congress Sanborn maps, NAIP and USGS historical aerials, HABS/HAER drawings, and **the owner's own photographs**. Photos of buildings visible from a public place are fine to take and use (17 USC 120(a)).
- **Measure, never trace or texture,** from everything else: NRHP nomination photos (third-party work, not federal), Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA), Flickr, Mapillary and KartaView (BY-SA), Hamilton County and TDOT imagery and vectors (no licence stated), and OpenStreetMap (ODbL). Taking a dimension from a photo is not an adaptation of it; baking the photo into a texture is.
- **Excluded from modelling:**
  - Google Street View, Google Earth and Google satellite imagery. Google's Geo Guidelines forbid "digitizing or tracing" from Street View and using Earth output "to reconstruct 3D models". Viewing them to decide _where to look_ is not modelling; putting them in a Match Photo scene is.
  - SketchUp Photo Textures and Add Location imagery. The EULA bars them from "mapping or geographic" apps, the same clause as #714.
  - Anything from 3D Warehouse. The ada repo's security rules forbid downloading from it anyway.
- **Colour-only materials** unless the texture comes from the owner's own photo.
- **Per model:** a `sources.json` listing every input with its licence, and the chosen licence in the GLB's `asset.copyright`.
- **Credit lines:** "Credit: U.S. Geological Survey"; "Library of Congress, Geography and Map Division, Sanborn Maps Collection".

**Open for the owner** (and recorded in #1289):

- **Tool.** SketchUp Make 2017's EULA (§2.2.1) is non-commercial, and its example of a disqualifying user ("a for-profit organization of any kind") arguably fits scripthammer.com, which sells services. Trimble claims no rights in the output, so the GLBs are clean either way; the risk is to the owner's own licence. The options are Make 2017 under a written posture (never sell a model, no client work), SketchUp Pro or Go, or Blender.
- **Output licence:** CC BY 4.0 (recommended) or CC0.
- **Credit name.**
- **Whether authored models may ship in the native Expo apps.**

## The backlog

[chatt-historic-backlog.md](chatt-historic-backlog.md) holds all 215 buildings: **59 tier A** (NRHP-listed or named landmark), **82 B**, **20 C** and **54 D**, plus 31 non-buildings. Each row gives the Warehouse slug(s) it replaces, an OpenStreetMap link and 1–4 sources. 13 buildings are demolished and can only be modelled from archival evidence; 38 have no model today. It is generated from the research JSON, not hand-typed: to change a row, correct it there and say so in the building's issue.

**Ranks 1–3 are one place.** The 1886 Car Barns building (demolished), R.H. Hunt's 1907 trolley barn across W 3rd (standing, and becoming a Mast General Store in spring 2027, so photograph it **before the renovation**), and the Car Barns' 1906 office wing (gone). After those, each tier is ordered by distance from the spawn.

## Building #1: the Chattanooga Car Barns

The full brief is [car-barns/BRIEF.md](car-barns/BRIEF.md), the measurements are [car-barns/massing_spec.json](car-barns/massing_spec.json), and the work is tracked in #1290. The short version:

- **The name misleads.** The owner asked for "the CARTA bus barn next to the Choo Choo". The historic CARTA bus barn is 1.8 km north of the Choo Choo, at Market & W 3rd by the Aquarium. The CARTA garage beside the Choo Choo is a modern parking deck (OSM way 45509897).
- **The 1979 NRHP listing (#79002436) covers three buildings.** Only the **1886-87 barn** south of W 3rd was demolished in 2025. Wikipedia's "built 1887, R.H. Hunt" conflates it with Hunt's **1907 trolley barn** north of W 3rd, which **still stands** (NRHP #80003806). The 1906 office and the 1926 garage have been gone since before 2008.
- **Measured:** 61.05 × 33.57 m (the NRHP says 200 × 110 ft), rotated 1.45° from true east. Grade 205.83–206.09 m NAVD88. A flat concrete deck falls 0.69 m to the north. The Market and Broad ends carry the same stepped parapet, peaking about 10.5 m above grade. The square chimney at the SW corner rises 13.6 m.
- **As of October 1978:** 4 car doors on each end, 10 bays of paired arched windows along W 3rd, and a one-storey bus-wash wing (1953/55) at the SW corner. Inside, one open repair floor with two rows of steel columns and two inspection pits.
- **Warehouse slot:** `300-market-street` (Chattanooga 3D, 2010). Its metadata names the Sports Barn, the gym the building became after CARTA.

## Replacing a Warehouse slot

**This does not work yet.** Three pieces of code have to change first, and #1290 tracks them:

1. **`scripts/warehouse/emit-models.ts` needs an authored source.** Today it projects Warehouse inventory only, and its sweep (lines 260-268) **deletes every served GLB not on its list**, so an own-authored model is wiped by the next `pnpm warehouse:sync`. Add a committed `scripts/warehouse/authored-chatt.json` (slug, file, title, creator, url, licence, lat, lon, yawDeg, neighborhood, replaces, hideBuildingIds). Emit it through the same `lonLatToEnu`, overrides and `boxesUnder`, and add it to the keep-list.
2. **Types and credits:**
   - `src/lib/manifest.ts:391-417`: make `warehouseId` optional and add `source` and `licence`.
   - `src/app/credits/page.tsx`: split into own-authored and 3D Warehouse sections.
   - `src/twin/SelectedBuildingCard.tsx`: stop hard-coding "View on 3D Warehouse".
   - `tests/unit/credits-page.test.ts:48-55` and `scripts/warehouse/__tests__/slugs.test.ts:103-105`: accept authored entries.
3. **Suppression.** `emit` hides OSM boxes within 10 m of the anchor. A demolished building may have nothing left to hide, while its **replacement** (a new hotel) will extrude on top of it when OSM maps it. So an authored entry needs an explicit `hideBuildingIds`. For the Car Barns, the neighbouring office (way 66418285) is mis-drawn 4.3–4.8 m into the barn and must be clipped, not hidden.

Then, per building:

- Exclude the Warehouse slug in `scripts/warehouse/overrides-chatt.json`, with a `_why` naming the issue.
- Add the authored entry. Place the anchor at the SketchUp origin (a surveyed corner), set `yawDeg` from the measured rotation, and fine-tune in `/chatt/?edit&select=<slug>`.
- Run `pnpm warehouse:sync`, then the warehouse and credits tests, then walk to the building in `/chatt/?diorama&walk`.
- Re-bake TurtleWolfe-Expo's `city.json` if the game should get it too.

**The default `/chatt` view is the Cesium atlas, and it draws no GLBs at all**, Warehouse or authored. It also has no suppression, so rebuilt buildings show only in `?diorama`. Making Cesium render authored GLBs, and hide the OSM boxes under them, is new code; open its issue when someone starts it.

## Traps found while researching (keep these)

- **Warehouse anchors are upload origins, not building positions.** Several "Chattanooga 3D" models share one anchor, and "Liberty Tower" anchors 700 m from Liberty Tower. The building is at `anchor + centerOffset` from `models.json`, and even that is a bounding-box centre that can include site plates hundreds of metres wide.
- **A dossier's printed coordinates can be wrong while its saved file is right.** The first Car Barns footprint printed in a report sat 66 m south, on the neighbouring office. The adversarial pass caught it. Take coordinates from the saved spec, not from prose.
- **The NRIS record is the root of the "R.H. Hunt, 1887" error.** It puts Hunt and 1887/1906/1926 on the whole three-building listing. Read the nomination text, not the database summary.
- **County assessor build years are unreliable here.** They give "built about 1920" for both the 1886 barn and the 1907 barn.
- **The Street View Static API serves only the newest capture.** At Market and Broad, the newest (2026-07) shows the demolition site. The only pre-demolition default pano is on W 3rd (2023-06). None of it is usable for modelling anyway (see the licence rules).
- **A research agent once sent the owner's email in a Nominatim `User-Agent`.** Use a generic agent string, such as `ScriptHammer-research/1.0 (+https://github.com/TortoiseWolfe/ScriptHammer)`, for every public API.
- **`grep` on this machine is `ugrep`**, and it silently skips some files. Use `command grep -r` for sweeps.
