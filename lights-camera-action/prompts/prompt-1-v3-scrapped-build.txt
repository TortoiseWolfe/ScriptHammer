Each instrument makes its own simple synthesized sound in the browser at the pitch written: a bass pluck, guitar strum, sax tone, piano note, trumpet blast, a sung "la," a synth blip, drum hits.

PLAYBACK: A big Play/Stop button and a tempo slider. When playing, a bright marker sweeps the 16 steps, every channel plays its programmed pattern in sync, and each robot animates when its own notes sound. The game starts with one complete song already programmed across all 8 channels so it sounds good the moment a kid presses Play — kids then open any channel and rearrange that instrument's part to make the song their own.
